US Political Test

Where do you stand
in American politics?

70 statements on the issues defining America right now: healthcare, immigration, abortion, guns, and the economy. Every position is asked twice with opposite framing, revealing both your party alignment and your framing bias.

Democrat
Your answers most closely match the Democratic Party platform. You tend to favour government intervention to reduce inequality, protect civil rights, and address climate change.
Republican
Your answers most closely match the Republican Party platform. You tend to favour lower taxes, strong borders, traditional values, and a powerful national defence.
Libertarian
Your answers most closely match the Libertarian Party platform. You want maximum freedom in both economic and personal life, with minimal government intervention in either.
Green
Your answers most closely match the Green Party platform. You prioritise environmental justice, corporate accountability, and a fundamental restructuring of the economy.

Statements, asked twice

You take a position on sharp political and ideological statements using a six-point agreement scale. Key positions appear twice with opposite framing, once sympathetic and once critical. How much your answer shifts between the two framings becomes your framing bias score.

Alignment, not loyalty

The result shows how closely your answers match each position across all issues, not just the ones you feel strongly about. You might find you align with a party or ideology you didn't expect, or that you're genuinely split between two.
