Where do you stand in British politics?

70 statements on the issues defining Britain right now: the NHS, immigration, Brexit, tax, crime, and net zero. Every position is asked twice with opposite framing, revealing both your party alignment and your framing bias.

Labour
Your answers most closely match the Labour Party. You tend to support public services, workers rights, and government intervention to reduce inequality.
Conservative
Your answers most closely match the Conservative Party. You tend to favour lower taxes, a smaller state, and traditional institutions.
Liberal Democrats
Your answers most closely match the Liberal Democrats. You favour individual freedom, strong public services, and closer ties with Europe.
Reform UK
Your answers most closely match Reform UK. You want radical cuts to immigration, NHS reform, and a rejection of the mainstream political consensus.
Restore Britain
Your answers most closely match Restore Britain. You support secure borders, low taxes, small government, national pride, and traditional Christian principles.
Green Party
Your answers most closely match the Green Party. You prioritise environmental action, wealth redistribution, and systemic economic change.
SNP
Your answers most closely match the SNP. You support Scottish independence, centre-left economics, and a closer relationship with Europe.

Statements, asked twice

You take a position on sharp political and ideological statements using a six-point agreement scale. Key positions appear twice with opposite framing, once sympathetic and once critical. How much your answer shifts between the two framings becomes your framing bias score.

Alignment, not loyalty

The result shows how closely your answers match each position across all issues, not just the ones you feel strongly about. You might find you align with a party or ideology you didn't expect, or that you're genuinely split between two.
