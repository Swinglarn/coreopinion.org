Where do you stand in
New Zealand politics?

70 statements on the issues defining New Zealand right now: the Treaty and co-governance, the cost of living, crime, housing, and tax. Every position is asked twice with opposite framing, revealing both your party alignment and your framing bias.

National
Your answers most closely match the National Party. You support tax cuts, fiscal responsibility, infrastructure investment, and a strong focus on law and order.
Labour
Your answers most closely match the Labour Party. You support social safety nets, investments in public healthcare and education, and state-backed climate action.
Green Party
Your answers most closely match the Green Party. You place high importance on aggressive carbon emission cuts, environmental protection, wealth redistribution, and social justice.
ACT
Your answers most closely match ACT New Zealand. You support classic liberal policies: a minimal state, tax simplification, deregulation, and individual liberties.
NZ First
Your answers most closely match New Zealand First. You support moderate protectionism, nationalist policies, stricter immigration controls, and support for seniors.
Te Pāti Māori
Your answers most closely match Te Pāti Māori. You support indigenous Māori rights and self-determination, environmental protection, and progressive social justice policies.

Statements, asked twice

You take a position on sharp political and ideological statements using a six-point agreement scale. Key positions appear twice with opposite framing, once sympathetic and once critical. How much your answer shifts between the two framings becomes your framing bias score.

Alignment, not loyalty

The result shows how closely your answers match each position across all issues, not just the ones you feel strongly about. You might find you align with a party or ideology you didn't expect, or that you're genuinely split between two.
