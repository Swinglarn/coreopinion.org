AU Political Test

Where do you stand in Australian politics?

70 statements on the issues defining Australia right now: Medicare, immigration, climate and energy, housing, and the cost of living. Every position is asked twice with opposite framing, revealing both your party alignment and your framing bias.

Labor
Your answers most closely match the Australian Labor Party. You favour workers rights, Medicare, clean energy transition, and a stronger role for government in reducing inequality.
Liberal
Your answers most closely match the Liberal Party. You favour lower taxes, smaller government, strong borders, and a market-led approach to the economy.
Nationals
Your answers most closely match the Nationals. You prioritise regional Australia, farming communities, resource industries, and scepticism of city-centric climate policy.
Greens
Your answers most closely match the Greens. You support treating climate as an emergency, ending fossil fuel extraction, wealth taxes, and strong renters rights.
Teal Independents
Your answers most closely match the Teal independents. You support strong integrity reforms, climate action, and pragmatic centrist economics, outside the major party blocs.

Statements, asked twice

You take a position on sharp political and ideological statements using a six-point agreement scale. Key positions appear twice with opposite framing, once sympathetic and once critical. How much your answer shifts between the two framings becomes your framing bias score.

Alignment, not loyalty

The result shows how closely your answers match each position across all issues, not just the ones you feel strongly about. You might find you align with a party or ideology you didn't expect, or that you're genuinely split between two.
